# SIH 2026: Consolidated Findings List

**Updated:** 2026-09-28  
**Purpose:** Separate actionable list of substantiated issues found in the SIH evaluator, mathematical, ML, business, and industry-grade reviews. Finding status is retained; resolved items are historical and should not be read as open defects. Detailed evidence and review-by-review discussion are in [the full audit](sih_2026_implementation_audit.md).

Of F-01–F-24, 12 findings are resolved at their stated scope and 12 remain open
or partially resolved. F-25 is an ML deployment blocker. F-26 is a new MPS
model-semantics release blocker identified in the current review.
